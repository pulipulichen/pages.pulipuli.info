Privacy Policy

Who operates this application

Web Dashboard (OAuth consent-screen name: WDB 20240106-1707) is maintained by pulipulichen (布丁布丁吃布丁). This notice covers the Google Apps Script–based Web Dashboard integration known as “Bill Records — ErisStar Billing Records — AppSheet 2026.” It is not a blanket policy for unrelated applications.

The pages on this website provide product and policy information only. They do not themselves request Google account authorization.

Google data that may be accessed

The billing-record workflow can use Google Drive folders, file identifiers, file names, folder metadata, and Google Sheets spreadsheets or cell values that are configured for the workflow. Depending on the contents of those spreadsheets, records may contain billing dates, amounts, transaction details, names, or other business information.

The Apps Script code currently includes a request for the broad Google Drive OAuth scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive). That scope can permit more access than a single folder. The actual files processed depend on the application's code and configuration. Authorization may be given by the account executing the Apps Script; visiting the published web app does not necessarily mean a visitor grants their own Google Drive access.

Purposes of processing

Google data is used to locate configured Drive resources and to retrieve, display, create, or update billing records and related spreadsheet-based workflows where those functions are enabled. Technical metadata and application error logs may be used to troubleshoot service failures.

The intended purpose of this integration is billing-record management, not behavioral advertising or sale of Google user data.

Storage, logs, and sharing

Operational files and records are held in the Google Drive and Google Sheets accounts and locations configured by the operator. Apps Script executions and Google Cloud logging may contain diagnostic information such as error messages or resource identifiers. The application operator controls configured documents and their sharing permissions.

Google processes information as the underlying cloud-service provider. Data may be visible to other people who have access to the relevant Drive files or spreadsheets, or to authorized application operators. No public access to billing files is implied by the existence of these public policy pages.

Retention and deletion

Billing documents remain in their configured Google accounts until the document owner or authorized administrator deletes or changes them, subject to applicable business record-retention requirements. Operational logs are retained according to the applicable Google Cloud or Apps Script settings. To request access, correction, or deletion of data managed by this integration, contact the operator using the email below.

Security and your choices

Access to Google resources is controlled through Google account permissions, OAuth scopes, and the Apps Script deployment configuration. Authorized users can review or revoke granted access through their Google Account security settings. Revoking access may stop functions that depend on Google Drive or Sheets. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children and changes

This is a business-oriented billing-record tool and is not intended for children. This notice may be updated if features, permissions, or data-handling practices change; the effective date will be updated with material changes.

Contact

For questions or privacy requests, email pulipuli.chen@gmail.com.

目前已知的 Apps Script 程式包含完整 Google Drive OAuth 權限（https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive）要求。此權限的技術授權範圍可能超過單一資料夾，實際處理的檔案則取決於程式邏輯與設定。授權可能由執行 Apps Script 的帳戶提供；造訪已部署的 Web App 不一定代表訪客授權自己的 Google Drive。

此整合功能的預定用途是管理帳務紀錄，不是行為廣告投放，也不是出售 Google 使用者資料。
